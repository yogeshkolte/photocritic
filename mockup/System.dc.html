Design system v1 · decision 36

Darkroom

The photo is the hero. Interface surfaces are near-black and quiet; one amber "safelight" accent marks the primary action. Glass only on controls over photos; any text sits on a tint of at least 65%. Dark theme only in v1. System fonts, Lucide icons.

App icon direction: aperture + speech tail. Dark and amber variants.
